Skill Development · AI Value Delivery Workshop

From undefined opportunity to a scalable, outcome-led AI MVP

Most AI initiatives start with a technology possibility rather than an owned business problem. This workshop puts business, product, design, data, AI, engineering and governance stakeholders into one working cadence — so the right choices get made early, and the team leaves with a delivery package, not a slide deck.

Workshop objectives

Seven decisions the room needs to make together

Each objective produces a specific artifact that the next objective depends on — this is a connected system, not a checklist.

01

Turn an ambiguous AI opportunity into a specific, agreed business problem with measurable outcomes and guardrails.

02

Design a solution that is desirable for users, viable for the business, feasible with available data and technology, and responsible to operate.

03

Create a realistic delivery forecast through progressive estimation, capacity awareness and transparent assumptions.

04

Optimize the delivery system around value flow by clarifying roles, reducing handoffs and managing dependencies proactively.

05

Align the near-term plan to OKRs and establish a big-room-planning cadence that makes decisions, risks and progress visible.

06

Build a coherent hierarchy from epics to features, stories and tasks, so implementation work is valuable, testable and appropriately sized.

07

Set a disciplined POC-to-MVP progression that balances speed of learning with the controls needed to scale.

Recommended format

Four days for the full design. Three, if you compress.

4 DAYS · FULL DISCOVERY-TO-DELIVERY DESIGN

Recommended when the objective is to complete the full path from framing through backlog and POC/MVP plan in one continuous cadence.

3 DAYS · COMPRESSED

Possible by combining solution design and delivery planning, with deeper refinement of the backlog and POC plan completed in a follow-on working session.

Suggested participants

One accountable sponsor. A small, empowered group.

The workshop is designed for approximately 10–18 active participants, supported by a broader stakeholder group at key decision points.

  • Sponsor & product ownerSet outcome ambition, make scope decisions, own the investment decision.
  • Process SMEs & usersContribute current-state context, pain points, needs and validation feedback.
  • Product, design, delivery leadsShape the experience, backlog, sequencing, measures and delivery cadence.
  • Data, AI, security, risk partnersConfirm feasibility, controls and scale-readiness constraints.
  • Engineering & operations leadsTest capacity, reliability, integration and operating-model implications.
Pre-work questionnaire

Six questions, answered 3–5 business days before the room convenes

All nominated attendees and key stakeholders complete this short questionnaire so the agenda can be tailored, the priority opportunity confirmed, and the right people brought in for the decisions that need them.

Business priority

What business problem or decision should this AI initiative address first?

Users & current state

Which users or personas are affected, and what are their most important pain points today?

Desired outcomes

What measurable business or customer outcome should improve? Include a baseline and target, if available.

AI opportunity & data

Where could AI assist, automate or augment decisions — and what data or systems are available or missing?

Constraints & readiness

What risks, dependencies, policy considerations or approvals need to be addressed?

Workshop expectations

What would make the workshop successful, and which questions or decisions need to be resolved?
